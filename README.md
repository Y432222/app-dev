## 📝 DESCRIPTION

 

Shameless follows the chaotic lives of the Gallagher family,

a dysfunctional, working-class family living on the South Side

of Chicago.

 

With an alcoholic and irresponsible father, the Gallagher

children are forced to raise themselves while dealing with

poverty, relationships, addiction, family conflicts, and

the harsh realities of life.

 

At the center of it all is Fiona Gallagher, the eldest sibling,

who sacrifices much of her youth to become the parent her

younger brothers and sisters never really had.

 

It's a story about survival, family, mistakes, love, and

growing up in a world that doesn't make things easy.
